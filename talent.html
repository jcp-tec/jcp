How we use your data

What we collect
Your name, email and phone number from this form, plus what you share later on WhatsApp, such as role, city, experience, salary expectation, availability and your resume.
Why
Only to evaluate your application and contact you about opportunities at Just Code People.
Legal basis
Your consent, in accordance with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law). We record the date and time you accepted.
Where it is stored
Your data is kept in a restricted Google Drive spreadsheet and the conversation takes place on WhatsApp. Only the people involved in our recruiting have access.
Your rights
You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by writing to info@justcodepeople.com.
